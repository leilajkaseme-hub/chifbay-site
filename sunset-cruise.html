01The experience

Golden hour,
west-bound

We leave Marina do Funchal at 18:30 and run west along the coast — past Câmara de Lobos, the fishing village Churchill came to paint, and out under Cabo Girão, 580 metres of sheer basalt dropping straight into the Atlantic. The drone goes up there, so you leave with the footage.

Drinks and food come out on deck, the engine drops to a hush and the light does the rest — gold, then copper, then deep red. The way home is the fun part: throttle open, spray flying, Funchal lighting up ahead.

03Last light

The best table in
Madeira has no floor

Madeira sunsets are dramatic because of the clouds — the colour comes from light hitting them. Slip out of the marina, watch the city turn amber behind you, and let the engine drop to a hush under the cliff.

This is a cruising trip, not a swimming one: by half six the water has lost the day's warmth, so we keep you on deck with a drink instead. If you want to get in — snorkel, jump, paddle — take the day trip instead, it's built around exactly that.

05Good to know

Questions, answered

What time does the sunset trip depart?+

Every sunset trip casts off at 18:30 from Marina do Funchal. The 2h option is back around 20:30, the 2h30 option around 21:00. Arrive about 15 minutes early to settle in.

What's the difference between the 2h and the 2h30?+

Only how far west we go. The 2h (€400) turns back at Cabo Girão. The 2h30 (€500) carries on past Fajã dos Padres to Ribeira Brava before turning. Everything on board is identical.

Can we swim on the sunset trip?+

No — swimming, jumping in and paddle are on the day trip only. By 18:30 the water is too cold to enjoy properly, so the sunset trip is a cruising trip: coastline, drone, drinks and food on deck.

What's included?+

Drinks and food aboard, drone footage of your trip, the private boat for up to 5 guests and a local skipper. One flat price for the boat — not per person.

What if it's cloudy and there's no sunset?+

Madeira sunsets are dramatic because of the clouds. Total overcast is rare — if it happens, we reschedule or refund. We watch the forecast obsessively.
