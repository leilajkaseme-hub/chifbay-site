## 3. Algorithmic Q&A Seeds for Google Maps

*Google Maps scans the Q&A section and bolds matching keywords when tourists search. You can ask and answer these directly on your Google Business Profile:*

### Q1: Is the boat tour completely private, or will there be other passengers?
**A:** Every Chifbay trip is 100% private. We only accept one booking at a time for up to 5 guests. You never share the boat with strangers — the boat, itinerary, and two local skippers belong entirely to your party.

### Q2: Where do we meet for departure in Funchal?
**A:** All charters depart from Marina do Funchal, Pontoon C (right next to the central statue), a 5-minute walk from Funchal’s historic old town and downtown hotels.

### Q3: What is included in the tour price?
**A:** Our flat rate covers the whole boat for up to 5 guests: two professional local skippers, fuel, insurance, on-board bathroom, complimentary wine, poncha, beer, soft drinks, fresh artisan snacks, and 4K drone video. On day trips, paddleboards and swim stops are also included.

### Q4: Can we swim at Fajã dos Padres?
**A:** Yes! Our Day Trip anchors inside the sheltered cove of Fajã dos Padres, where guests can jump off the swim platform into crystal-clear turquoise waters and use our stand-up paddleboards.

### Q5: How much does a private boat tour cost?
**A:** Pricing is a flat rate for the entire boat (not per person): €400 for the 2-hour sunset cruise, €500 for the 2.5-hour day or sunset trip, and €600 for the 3-hour day trip including an edited 4K video.

### Q6: Might we see dolphins or whales during the trip?
**A:** Yes, dolphins and pilot whales are frequently encountered along Madeira's south coast. Whenever marine life appears, our skipper idles the engine to observe them respectfully from a safe distance.

### Q7: Is there a bathroom on board?
**A:** Yes, our 2026 Karnic R8S has an enclosed private cabin with an electric marine toilet and fresh water sink, plus an aft freshwater deck shower.

### Q8: How far in advance should we book?
**A:** Because Chifbay operates a single private boat with dedicated local skippers, slots during peak season (May through October) fill up quickly. Real-time availability is always visible on our live calendar at chifbay.com/book.html.
