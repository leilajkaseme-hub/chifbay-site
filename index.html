01The starting point

Funchal, where Madeira
meets the open Atlantic

Madeira rises from the ocean like a green amphitheatre, and its capital Funchal sits right at the water's edge — the natural place to begin any exploration by sea.

A private trip with Chifbay casts off from the marina aboard our 2026 Karnic R8S. No queues, no strangers, no fixed script — just your group, a skipper who knows every cove, and a route built entirely around you.

From the sea the scale is a different thing entirely — a sheer wall of volcanic basalt rising 580 metres straight from the Atlantic, one of the highest sea cliffs in Europe. The route also passes Câmara de Lobos, the fishing village that drew Winston Churchill to paint.

08Good to know

Questions, answered

What's the best way to see Madeira's south coast by boat?+

A private charter, not a shared group tour. Chifbay takes one group at a time — up to 5 guests — on a 2026 Karnic R8S with two skippers, departing Marina do Funchal. Choose a day trip with swimming and paddle at Fajã dos Padres, or a sunset cruise along the same coast.

How much does a private boat tour cost in Madeira?+

From €400 for the 2-hour sunset trip to €600 for the 3-hour day trip — one flat price for the whole boat, not per person, for up to 5 guests.

Is a private charter more expensive than a shared boat tour?+

For two people, yes, and the gap closes as the group grows. The price is for the whole boat, from €400: €200 each for two, €100 each for four, €80 each for five. You get the boat, the route and the schedule to yourselves.

What boat do you use, and how many guests can it carry?+

A 2026 Karnic R8S — 9 metres, Mercury V8 300hp, cushioned seating and a swim platform — with two Chifbay skippers on board every trip. Up to 5 guests, private, never shared with another group.

Is there a bathroom on board?+

Yes — there's a bathroom on board.

What food and drinks are included?+

Local wine, poncha, beer, soft drinks and snacks, served on deck — included in the price on every trip, no extra charge.

Where do trips depart from?+

Marina do Funchal, Pontoon C, next to the statue — on Madeira's south coast, a few minutes' walk from the old town. Arrive 15 minutes before departure.

Might we see dolphins or whales?+

Often, though it's never guaranteed — dolphins are seen regularly off Madeira's south coast and your skipper will slow down for a good look whenever they show up. It's wildlife, not a scripted stop, so timing is down to the ocean, not us.
