Is a Private Boat Trip in Madeira Worth the Money?

By Chifbay13 November 20265 min readFunchal · Madeira

Usually yes for a group of four or five, who pay 80 to 150 EUR each for a boat that is entirely theirs. For a solo traveller on a budget, a seat on a shared catamaran is the better deal.

What does it cost per person?

Less than most people expect once the group is full. The price is per boat, up to 5 guests, so the cost per head falls with every person you add. Here is the maths for the four options.

For comparison, shared catamaran trips from Funchal are sold by the seat, and on the big booking sites they are usually listed somewhere around 40 to 50 EUR per person for about three hours. With five on board, a private boat comes to roughly two to three times the price of a shared seat per head. A couple on their own pays four to seven times as much.

What do you get that a shared trip does not give you?

The short answer is control of the space and a route built for swimming and photos, not for the most passengers. On a Chifbay trip, the price covers:

  • No strangers on deck, and a local professional skipper who works for your group alone.
  • Food served on board, with local wine, poncha, beer and soft drinks to go with it.
  • Pictures of your own group: real camera photos, a 360° Insta360 video, and drone shots on days the wind allows.
  • Your own bathroom on board.
  • On the Day Trip, time in the water at Fajã dos Padres and Ribeira Brava, jumping off the boat and paddle boarding.

The route is the south coast west of Funchal: Câmara de Lobos, then the 580 metre wall of Cabo Girão seen from its foot, then the swim stops, then a fast run home. The 3 hour Day Trip adds 30 minutes further west to Ponta do Sol. On a shared boat you might see much of the same coast, but you will see it with a deck full of strangers, and the swim, if there is one, runs to a group timetable.

Add the photos and video, and part of the price replaces something you might otherwise pay a photographer for, or go home without.

Who is a private boat clearly worth it for?

For these groups, we think the private boat wins easily.

  • Groups of four or five. At 80 to 150 EUR each, you are paying a moderate premium over a shared seat for a boat that is entirely yours.
  • Couples marking something. A proposal, an anniversary or a honeymoon is not a moment you want to share with a deck full of strangers.
  • Swimmers. If the main reason you want to go to sea is to jump into deep blue water under the cliffs, the Day Trip is built around that.
  • People who want the photos. Real camera photos, a 360° video and drone footage are included, and they are all of your group.
  • Cruise passengers on a port day. A Day Trip of 2 hours 30 leaving at 10:00 is back by 12:30, with the afternoon free for Funchal.

Who should take a big catamaran instead?

Honestly, a private boat is not the right choice for everyone, and we would rather say so here than have you disappointed on board.

  • Solo travellers on a budget. One person pays for the whole boat. A shared seat is a fraction of the price.
  • Groups of more than five. The boat takes up to 5 guests. A bigger group needs a bigger boat, or two bookings.
  • People who get seasick easily. A large catamaran usually moves less than a small motor boat. If motion is your worry, read our guide to seasickness on Madeira boat trips before you choose.
  • Anyone whose main goal is dolphins and whales. We make no promises about wildlife. If a long search for dolphins is the whole point, a dedicated whale-watching trip is designed for that. Our guide to dolphins and whales in Madeira explains what you can realistically see.
  • Travellers who like a crowd. Some people enjoy the music and chatter of a full deck. That is a fair choice too.

Does the season change the answer?

A little. In summer the Sunset Trip leaves around 20:00 and the evening is warm, so even a 2 hour trip feels generous. In December it leaves around 16:45, and the evening cools fast once the sun has gone, so bring a layer. The Day Trip is at its best when the water is warm enough that everyone wants to swim. In the cooler months fewer people get in, and a group that will not swim may get more for its money from the Sunset Trip. If you want to compare the two formats side by side, our private charter versus shared tour comparison goes deeper than this page.

Are there extra costs to add on top?

No. On a Chifbay booking, the price per boat is the price of the trip, with food, drinks, photos and video included. Tipping the skipper is entirely optional. The one thing to add is getting to the marina, and that is often a walk: it sits halfway along the Funchal seafront, close to the old town and the centre.

How do you decide in one minute?

  1. Count your group. At four or five, the private boat is close to shared-trip money per head.
  2. Ask what you want most: swimming and space, or the lowest price.
  3. Check your dates. A booking can be dropped without charge up to 24 hours ahead, and a trip the skipper cancels for sea conditions moves to a new date or is refunded in full.
  4. Choose the trip. Swims mean the Day Trip west to Fajã dos Padres. Golden light without swimming means the Sunset Trip.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a private boat trip in Madeira per person?

It depends on group size. With five guests it is 80 to 120 EUR each. With two it is 200 to 300 EUR each.

Is a shared catamaran cheaper than a private boat?

Yes, per person. Shared seats are usually listed at around 40 to 50 EUR each, but you share the boat with many others.

What is included in the price of a private boat?

Food, drinks, real camera photos, a 360° video, drone footage when the weather allows, and a bathroom on board. The Day Trip adds swims and paddle boards.

Can more than five people book one private boat?

No. The boat takes up to 5 guests.

Five people, one boat, 80 EUR each: the sum that turns a treat into the obvious choice.
