Can You Watch Madeira's New Year Fireworks From a Boat?

By Chifbay9 November 20265 min readFunchal · Madeira

You can, and the water is the best seat in town. Funchal's New Year fireworks are fired at midnight from the hillsides, the seafront and boats around the bay, so only the sea faces the whole display at once. On 31 December 2026 Chifbay runs a fireworks night on the boat from 22:00 to 01:00.

What happens over Funchal at midnight?

At midnight on 31 December the whole bay of Funchal lights up for about eight minutes. The city is built like an amphitheatre, rising from the seafront up steep hills, and the firing points are spread across it. The show is not one spot in the sky but a wide arc of colour around the water.

The last show gives the scale. To welcome 2026, the city fired 97,000 shots in eight minutes from 58 positions on the hillsides, along the seafront and on boats in the bay, using 22 tonnes of pyrotechnic material, according to the local press. The plan for 31 December 2026 lists 59 launch sites.

The tradition has a record behind it. For the night of 31 December 2006, Guinness World Records recognised Funchal's display as the largest fireworks show in the world, with 66,326 fireworks. The shows have grown since.

The bay is busy too. Eleven cruise ships came to Funchal for the last New Year, and local press counted more than 30,000 people arriving by sea for the night. Several ships wait off the city for the show, lit from bow to stern, and they become part of the view.

Why is the sea the best seat?

From the water you look at the city instead of standing inside it. On the seafront you see the fireworks nearest to you and a lot of other people's heads. From the bay you see the full amphitheatre: the hills, rockets climbing from dozens of points at once, the colour doubled on the water, the ships around you.

There are practical reasons too:

  • No crowd. The seafront fills hours before midnight. On the Chifbay boat the deck holds only your group, up to 5 guests.
  • A seat, a bathroom and a drink. Nobody stands for two hours to keep a place by the railing.
  • The hours before. Watching the city's lights and the ships from the water before midnight is half the pleasure of the night.

There is one honest catch. Smoke goes where the wind goes. On a night with a light wind blowing out to sea, the later minutes of the show can look hazier from the water than from the hills. Nobody can plan around that weeks ahead.

How does the night on the boat run?

Our New Year's Eve fireworks night on the boat runs from 22:00 to 01:00 on 31 December 2026. The page has the price, what is included and how to book. The outline is simple:

  1. 21:45. Be on the pontoon at Marina do Funchal, in the middle of the seafront, where the skipper meets you. Allow extra time to walk through the crowds.
  2. 22:00. The boat leaves the marina. You have two hours on the water before the show.
  3. Before midnight. The skipper picks a place in the bay with a clear view and a safe distance from the firing boats and the cruise ships.
  4. Midnight. About eight minutes of fireworks around the whole bay.
  5. 01:00. Back at the marina.

As on every trip, the safety call is the skipper's. If the sea is not safe that night, we cancel and you choose a full refund or another date. For a night that happens once a year, the refund is the realistic choice. Keep one more fact in mind: the city fired the last show despite an orange weather warning, so fireworks going ahead does not mean the sea is right for a small boat.

How do you get to the marina on the night?

On foot, if you can. The marina is in the middle of the seafront, and on New Year's Eve that seafront is one of the busiest places on the island. Staying in central Funchal makes the night easy. If you stay outside the city, plan the way in before the day, and plan the way back after 01:00 too, because the whole city starts moving at once when the show ends.

What is it like on the water on New Year's Eve?

Mild by European standards, but cooler than the date on land suggests. Sunset on 31 December is around 18:12, so the whole trip is in the dark, and wind on the water takes heat from you fast.

  • Bring a warm layer and a windproof jacket, even if Funchal felt warm at lunch.
  • Wear closed shoes with grip. Smart shoes and a wet deck do not mix.
  • Winter brings Atlantic weather systems, so watch the forecast in the last days of December. Our guide on how cold it gets on the water in the evening helps with what to wear.

Is it a good night for children?

It depends on your group. The fireworks are loud, the night is long and the trip ends at 01:00. If you want to bring young children, ask us on WhatsApp before booking, with their ages, because a late night full of bangs is a lot for a small child. If you get seasick easily, a winter sea in the dark is harder on the stomach than a summer afternoon.

If you would rather keep your feet on land, the hills above the city give a wide view too, with the crowds that come with it. Our guide to Christmas and New Year in Madeira 2026 covers the land options and the rest of the season.

Why book early?

Because there is one boat and one New Year's Eve. Chifbay trips are private, so the night sells once, to one group of up to 5 people. The whole island also fills up for the fireworks: for New Year 2024, local press reported hotel occupancy close to 100 per cent.

The order that works is simple. Fix your flights and your room, then book the boat the same week. Leave it until December and the night may already be gone.

If the night is taken, the season still has evenings worth having on the water. A Sunset Trip in the last week of December leaves just before 17:00 and comes back after dark, with the Christmas lights on across the hills.
