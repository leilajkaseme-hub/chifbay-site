Romantic Boat Trip in Madeira

4 October 20265 min readFunchal · Madeira

Madeira is made for celebrations at sea: calm south-coast water, towering cliffs and sunsets that turn the whole sky gold. If you're planning a proposal, an anniversary or a quiet escape for two, here is how a private boat trip works and how to get the most from it.

Why is a private boat trip so good for a proposal in Madeira?

A private charter means the boat is yours alone — no strangers, no audience, no schedule but your own. On a shared tour, the big moment happens in front of a crowd. On a private boat from Marina do Funchal, it happens with just your crew, who know when to look the other way and when to pick up the camera.

The setting does the rest. The south coast offers sheltered water, the 580-metre wall of Cabo Girão and the colourful roofs of Câmara de Lobos in the background. It is hard to find a more cinematic backdrop on the island.

What is the best trip for a romantic occasion?

The Sunset Trip is the natural choice, departing 1 h 15 before sunset as the light softens. It follows the same coast as the Day Trip but without swimming, because the water is too cold that late. You can choose 2 hours (€400), turning at Cabo Girão, or 2h30 (€500), going on to Ribeira Brava. Drinks and food are aboard, and a drone films the boat against the cliffs.

Prefer daylight and swimming? The Day Trip leaves at 10:00 or 14:00, from €500 for 2h30 (€600 for 3h, continuing to Ponta do Sol). It pairs Cabo Girão with swim stops at Fajã dos Padres and Ribeira Brava — ideal for an adventurous anniversary rather than a candlelit one. Not sure which fits? What does a romantic trip with Chifbay actually look like?

You board at Marina do Funchal, settle in with drinks and food, and head west along the coast. The crew handles the boat; you handle nothing. On the Sunset Trip you reach Cabo Girão as the cliff catches the last light, then turn back as Funchal begins to glow. A drone captures the boat against the coast, so you have footage that looks like a film still rather than a phone snap.

Whether you stay seated with a glass in hand or walk to the bow for the moment itself is entirely up to you. There are no crowds and no one is waiting for your spot.

How do you plan a proposal at sea without it going wrong?

Keep it simple and tell the crew early. A few practical tips:

  • Message ahead. Let the team know it's a proposal so they can plan the timing and the best position for the drone.
  • Choose the moment. The golden-hour stretch near Cabo Girão is the visual peak; ask the crew to signal when the light is best.
  • Secure the ring. Boats move. Keep the ring in a zipped pocket until the moment, and stay steady when you take it out.
  • Mind your footing. Wear flat, soft-soled shoes and wait for calm water before you stand.
  • Keep the date flexible. Sunsets are strongest with some high cloud, and a flexible date helps if sea conditions change.

What should you wear and bring?

Dress for the sea, not the restaurant. A light layer matters because the wind picks up as the sun drops, even in summer. Sunglasses help on the Day Trip, and a swimsuit is needed if you plan to swim at Fajã dos Padres or Ribeira Brava. Bring your phone for stills; the drone handles the cinematic shots.

When is the best time of year?

Summer and early autumn bring the warmest water and the latest sunsets, but Madeira's mild climate means a sunset at sea is possible nearly year-round. Autumn light is gentler and the island is quieter. Book early for weekends in peak season.

The most romantic thing about Madeira isn't a place — it's having the Atlantic, the cliffs and the sunset entirely to yourselves.

A private boat trip turns a good evening into a story you'll retell for years. Pick your slot, tell the crew what you're planning, and let the coast do the work.
