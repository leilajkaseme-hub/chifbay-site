Practical information

Where to meet, when you leave and come back, what is on board and what to bring.

Meeting point

Marina do Funchal, pontoon side

Every trip leaves from Marina do Funchal, in the middle of the seafront. Your skipper meets you on the pontoon. Be there 15 minutes before departure.

Marina do Funchal, 9000-055 Funchal, Madeira, Portugal · 32.6442° N, 16.9165° W

On board

Included in every trip

  • The whole boat for your group, up to 5 guests
  • Two local skippers
  • Local wine, poncha, beer, soft drinks and snacks
  • Drone footage, an Insta360 video and photos of your trip
  • A bathroom on board
  • Paddle boards and swimming on the day trip
What to bring

Pack light

  • Swimwear and a towel for the day trip
  • Sun cream and sunglasses
  • A light jacket for the sunset trip
  • Your phone, to find the pontoon
Weather and cancellation

If plans change

  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before departure.
  • If the sea is not safe we cancel, and you choose another date or get a full refund.
  • Payment is by card for the whole boat, never per person.
