01Why from the water

The whole bay lights up.
You sit in the middle of it

At midnight on 31 December, Funchal fires its New Year fireworks from points all around the bay at once. The city is built like an amphitheatre on the hills, so the show makes a full circle of light. It was once named the largest fireworks display in the world by Guinness World Records, and the island still treats the night as its biggest party of the year.

On land, the seafront is packed from early evening and the streets close. From the boat you see the whole circle at once, with nobody in front of you, the colours doubled on the water and the ships in the harbour sounding their horns at midnight. Just your group, two skippers and a table full of Madeiran food and drink.

05Good to know

New Year's Eve on the boat: questions

When do the Madeira New Year fireworks start, and how long do they last?+

At midnight, from 31 December to 1 January. The show lasts about eight minutes and fires from many points around Funchal bay at the same time, so the whole city lights up in a circle. The ships in the harbour sound their horns at midnight.

Why watch the fireworks from a boat?+

The fireworks go up all around the bay, so the best seat is in the middle of it. On land the seafront is full from early evening and the streets close; on the boat you have the whole view, no crowd, and the reflections on the water.

What is included in the €3,000?+

Everything on this page: the whole boat for three hours, two skippers, a Madeiran dinner on deck, Champagne at midnight, poncha, Madeira wine, Portuguese wine, beer and soft drinks, Insta360 360° video of the night, drone shots before the show, camera photos and blankets. The price is for the whole boat, from 1 to 5 guests.

What if the weather is bad?+

The skippers decide on the day, for your safety. If the sea is too rough or the port authority stops boats from going out, you get a full refund. We tell you as early as we can.

Is it cold on the water at night in December?+

Funchal is mild in winter, around 15 °C at night, but it feels cooler on the water. Bring a warm layer and a jacket. Blankets are on board.

Can children come?+

Yes, of any age. There are life jackets in every size. The boat takes 5 guests in total, children included.

Do you have vegetarian or alcohol free options?+

Yes. Tell us when you book (we write to you on WhatsApp after payment) and we adapt the menu and the drinks.

Where do we meet, and how do we get back?+

Marina do Funchal, Pontoon C, at 21:45. We are back at 01:00 in the centre of Funchal, a short walk from the main hotels. Taxis are busy on New Year's night: book one ahead, or ask us and we help.

Can I cancel?+

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before departure.
