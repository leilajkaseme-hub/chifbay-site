01Four kinds of trip

Which boat trip suits you?

Madeira has four kinds of boat trip, and they are sold side by side on the same booking pages. The first thing to check is the unit: a shared trip is priced per person, a private boat is priced for the whole boat.

Per person · median €59, most €40 to €79

Shared catamaran or boat

A big boat on a fixed route and timetable, often with dozens of other guests. The cheapest way onto the water, and the right pick for one or two people on a budget.

Per person · about €45 to €67

Whale and dolphin watching

Trips built around finding marine life, by catamaran, traditional boat or fast RIB. Pick this if the animals are the reason you are going.

Per person or per boat

Sailing

Slower and quieter, under sail when the wind allows. Shared sailing trips and private sailing boats both exist; private sunset sails start around €500 per boat.

Per boat · median €537

Private boat

The whole boat for your group, with a skipper. You choose the pace, stay longer to swim, and nobody else is aboard. Prices run from about €150 to €3,500 per boat.

03Who each one suits

Pick by who is going

  • One or two people on a budget: a shared catamaran or a shared whale watching trip. A private boat makes little sense for one person.
  • You came for whales and dolphins: a specialist whale watching operator such as VMT Madeira or Lobosonda. That is their whole trip.
  • A couple celebrating, or a proposal: a private sunset trip. Nobody else aboard, and the timing follows the sun.
  • A family or a group of 4 or 5 who want to swim: a private day trip. The boat waits while you swim, and the price per person drops fast with a full boat.
  • A bigger group, 6 to 12 people: a private operator with a larger boat, such as Cristal Sea Madeira. Chifbay takes 5 guests at most.
  • You want wind and quiet: a sailing trip.
04Why a private boat

When a private boat is worth it

A private boat costs more than a shared seat. What you buy is control: you choose when to stop, how long to swim, whether to go fast or slow, and nobody else is on board. On the south-west coast, that means time at the base of Cabo Girão and a swim at Fajã dos Padres, the cove with no road in, without a timetable.

At Chifbay the whole boat is €400 for the 2 h sunset cruise and €500 or €600 for the day trip, for up to 5 guests, with two local skippers, food and drinks, a 360° video, drone footage and camera photos included. More detail on our private boat tour page and in private boat or catamaran?

05Before you book

Four questions to ask any operator

  1. Is the price per person or for the whole boat? Both appear in the same search results.
  2. How many other people will be aboard? A shared trip can mean 10 or 100.
  3. What is not in the price? Drinks, food, photos and hotel pickup are often extras.
  4. Where does it actually go, and for how long? Two trips of the same length can cover very different coast.
06Questions

Boat trips in Madeira: questions

What is the best boat trip in Madeira?+

It depends on who is going. For whales and dolphins, a specialist whale watching trip. For the lowest price per person, a shared catamaran. For a couple, a family or a small group who want to swim, take their time and have the boat to themselves, a private boat. For wind and quiet, a sailing trip.

How much does a boat trip in Madeira cost?+

When we counted every GetYourGuide boat listing for Madeira on 11 August 2026, the median shared trip was €59 per person (34 listings) and the median private boat was €537 for the whole boat (24 listings). Chifbay charges €400 to €600 for the whole boat, up to 5 guests.

Is a private boat cheaper than a shared trip for a group?+

Usually not. For two people a €400 private boat is €200 each against about €59 each on a shared trip. The gap closes as the group grows: €80 each for five. You pay for having the boat, the route and the time to yourselves.

Are dolphins and whales guaranteed?+

No trip can promise wild animals. Specialist whale watching operators go out to look for them, and that is their main focus. On a private coastal trip like ours, dolphins are a bonus when they show up, never a promise.

Where do boat trips leave from in Madeira?+

Most leave from Marina do Funchal. Some whale watching companies work from other towns, for example Lobosonda in Calheta, on the west of the south coast.

Which boat trip goes to Cabo Girão and Fajã dos Padres?+

Several trips pass Cabo Girão. Chifbay goes to Cabo Girão on every trip and stops to swim at Fajã dos Padres on the day trip.
