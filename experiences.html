Questions, answered

What's the difference between the Day Trip and the Sunset Trip?+

The Day Trip runs late morning or afternoon (10:00 or 14:00), includes swimming and paddle at Fajã dos Padres, and costs €500–€600. The Sunset Trip departs 18:30, is a cruising trip with no swimming — the water's too cold by evening — and costs €400–€500. Same boat, same coast, same skippers, just a different time of day.

Which trip should I choose?+

Choose the Day Trip to swim, jump in and paddle board. Choose the Sunset Trip for golden-hour light, drinks on deck and a quieter pace. Both cover the same stretch of coast toward Cabo Girão.

Is a private charter really better than a shared group tour?+

For most groups, yes. You get the whole boat — no waiting for other guests, no fixed script, and the route can flex around what your group wants to see. The price is a flat rate for the boat, not per person, so it's often the same or better value than shared tickets once you're two or more.

Can I build a fully custom route instead?+

Yes — message the skipper directly with your group size, dates and what you'd love to see (dolphins, coves, cliffs, sunset) and Chifbay will shape a private route around it.

How far ahead should I book?+

As early as you can, especially in high season — one boat means limited slots each day. Live availability is on the booking calendar for both trips.
