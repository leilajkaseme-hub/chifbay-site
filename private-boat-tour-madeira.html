01Key Specifications

The private charter
standard in Funchal

Unlike shared catamaran excursions carrying 50 to 100 passengers, Chifbay runs strictly private charters. You book the boat for your party alone. Two skippers attend to your group, the music is yours, and the pace adapts to where the sea and your curiosity take you.

Departing from Marina do Funchal (Pontoon C), we navigate Madeira’s towering south-west volcanic coastline — passing historic Câmara de Lobos, the 580m cliff of Cabo Girão, and the hidden bay of Fajã dos Padres.

Tour Overview at a Glance

  • Boat: 2026 Karnic R8S (9m, Mercury V8 300hp, swim platform, bathroom on board)
  • Capacity: 1 to 5 guests (100% private, never mixed groups)
  • Inclusions: Poncha, local wine, beer, soft drinks, artisan snacks, drone video
  • Water Activities: Paddleboards and swimming at Fajã dos Padres (Day Trip)
  • Pricing: Flat whole-boat rate from €400 to €600 (not per person)
  • Crew: Two experienced local skippers on every journey

04Frequently Asked Questions
What is the best way to see Madeira's south coast by boat?+

A private boat charter gives you an uninterrupted experience without the noise and crowded decks of commercial catamarans. Chifbay departs from Marina do Funchal and takes you right along the dramatic cliffs of Cabo Girão to hidden bays like Fajã dos Padres.

How much does a private boat tour cost in Madeira?+

Pricing is a flat rate for the whole boat (up to 5 guests): from €400 for the 2-hour sunset cruise to €600 for the 3-hour day trip with filmed 4K video. There are no hidden fees, fuel charges, or per-person markups.

What boat do you use, and is there a bathroom on board?+

You sail on a 2026 Karnic R8S (9 metres, Mercury V8 300hp) equipped with deep cushioned seating, sun loungers, an aft swim platform with ladder, fresh water shower, and a private enclosed bathroom cabin.

What food and drinks are included?+

Every charter includes complimentary local Madeiran wine, homemade poncha, Portuguese beer, fresh soft drinks, and artisan local snacks served on deck.

Where do trips depart from?+

Marina do Funchal, Pontoon C, next to the statue. It is located directly along the Funchal waterfront, just a 5-minute walk from the Old Town and major downtown hotels.

Might we see dolphins or whales?+

Yes, dolphins and pilot whales frequently visit Madeira's south-west coast year-round. While wildlife is never guaranteed, your skipper will always adjust speed and course for respectful observation whenever marine life is spotted.
